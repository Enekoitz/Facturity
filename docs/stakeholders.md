# Stakeholders

> **Purpose:** Identifying who has an interest in or influence over the project, and how that relationship is managed.
>
> **Guiding questions:** Who uses the system? Who pays for it? Who operates it? Who has the power to block it? Who is affected by it, even though they didn’t ask for it?

| **Stakeholder**                        | **Role**                       | **Expectations / Priorities**                                                                         | **Influence** | **Interest** | **Communication Method & Timing**                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **End User (Accountants / Admins)** | System operator          | Fast processes, an intuitive interface, zero technical errors, and responsive support if issues arise. | Low           | High        | **Channel:** Release notes, in-app notifications, and user guides.<br><br>  <br><br>**Frequency:** With every update or change to a workflow. |
| **Tax & Legal Team**              | Regulatory advisors           | Strict compliance with tax laws, valid file formats (XML/PDF), and no penalties. | High           | High        | **Channel:** Requirement review meetings and documentation.<br><br>  <br><br>**Frequency:** During the initial design phase and whenever there are legislative changes. |
| **Product Manager / Owner**            | Product leadership         | On-time delivery, user value, retention (low churn), and control over development costs. | High           | High        | **Channel:** Daily stand-ups, end-of-sprint demos, and KPI dashboards.<br><br>  <br><br>**Frequency:** Continuous / Weekly. |
| **Technical Team (Devs / QA)**         | Development & Quality Assurance          | Clear requirements, clean architecture, stable API integration, and maintainable code. | Medium          | High        | **Channel:** Backlog refinements, API documentation (Swagger), and Slack/Teams.<br><br>  <br><br>**Frequency:** Daily during development phases. |
| **Digital Stamping Provider (PAC / API)**  | External certification entity | Strict adherence to their SLA, well-structured requests, and a low error rate per call. | High           | Medium       | **Channel:** Official technical documentation and B2B support/ticketing system.<br><br>  <br><br>**Frequency:** Ad hoc (during integration or in the event of major incidents). |
